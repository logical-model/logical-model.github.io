Overview of logical

logical produces quantitative predictions from the logical model of minority representation developed in Atsusaka (2021).

What are logical models?

A logical model starts with a transparent account of the conditions that make an outcome possible. Here, candidate emergence and electoral success depend on the electoral environment facing a minority candidate and the minority share of the electorate. The package turns those quantities and explicit assumptions into probabilities, simulations, and visual comparisons; it does not replace substantive knowledge about local candidates, institutions, or elections.

The core inputs are M, the adjusted racial margin of victory, and C, the minority share of the electorate. Both use a 0–100 scale. Turnout rates, coethnic voting, and White crossover voting use a 0–1 scale.

First prediction

library(logical)
minorep(M = 50, C = 70)

minorep() returns a full-precision probability. Continue to Theory for the model, or select an application tab for a focused workflow.
