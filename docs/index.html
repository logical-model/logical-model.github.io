logical

Quantitative predictions from the logical model of minority representation.

The logical R package estimates and visualizes the probability that a minority candidate emerges and wins. It uses the adjusted racial margin of victory (M) and the minority share of the electorate (C).

Start with the overview and theory, then choose the application that matches your research question.
