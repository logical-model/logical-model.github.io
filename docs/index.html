A logical model of minority representation

logical is an R package for examining quantitative predictions from a logical model of minority representation. It connects electoral conditions within a district to the probability that a minority candidate emerges and wins.

What the model does

The model combines an adjusted racial margin of victory (M) with the minority share of the electorate (C) to estimate the probability of minority candidate emergence and electoral success. It supports district-level predictions, turnout adjustments, jurisdiction-level simulations, and comparisons of redistricting scenarios.

Pr(minority candidate emerges and wins) = Φ(√(M × C) − 50)
  • M is derived from the top minority and White candidate vote shares in the previous election.
  • C is the minority electorate percentage and may be adjusted for relative turnout.
  • Coethnic and crossover voting assumptions support prospective redistricting scenarios.

The package is designed to make model assumptions transparent. Results should be interpreted as model-based predictions, not as a guaranteed accuracy rate for every new election or district plan.
